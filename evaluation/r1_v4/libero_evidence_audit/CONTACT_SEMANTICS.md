# Geometry, ownership and initial support

See OBJECT_GEOMETRY_AUDIT.csv (20 object/boundary rows), SURFACE_GEOMETRY_AUDIT.csv (24 surface-piece rows), DIAGNOSTIC_WINDOWS.csv and the exact source hashes in SOURCE_BINDINGS.json.

The corrected native map resolves geom ID to body ID, ancestry, collision role, type, half-sizes and local transforms. cream_cheese collision ID104 belongs to body22; ketchup IDs131–142/body24; orange juice144–147/body25; milk149–152/body26; butter154/body27. All five are rigid free-joint bodies in this model. A root pose is neither center of mass nor a collision envelope. Compute each BOX's eight corners with its recorded world rotation/translation and native half-sizes, then union the pieces. Do not replace a complex object with one root-centered box.

Table visual mesh ID9 cannot serve as collision support. IDs10–15 are all table collision boxes; ID15 is lower structure, not interchangeable with the five upper tabletop faces. Historical V3 omitted15; frozen V3 stays unchanged. All six recorded transforms are exactly invariant across these saved exports. Restored model/body hierarchy is still needed to re-attest static-root configuration. BOX bounds in the CSV are geometry observations, not contact/support thresholds.

Native contacts are geometric constraint candidates. Signed distance and position do not identify a load-bearing force or its direction. Ownership permits distinguishing table, object, floor and robot geometry; it does not prove stable support, holding, intention or causation. Complete contact absence requires a verified complete recorder bundle and a profile that excludes alternative support/constraints. Export lists lack raw ncon, so completeness is inherited rather than freshly certified.

At frame10 the exported cream-cheese and butter contact patterns generally provide table-support candidates, while the three elevated objects have empty contact sets and separated collision bounds. Episode002 cream cheese is already below the tabletop. These reproduce measurement patterns documented in the prior audit; they do not independently validate support or assign new applicability. Prior setup-flight explanation remains provisional physical interpretation.

DIAGNOSTIC_WINDOWS.csv preserves cream-cheese geometry/contact values around episode002 setup departure and episode014 handling/release. Two contact-free descending saved shapes after bilateral contacts are candidates for a post-lift event; admission still needs validated support, handling, origin, uncertainty and boundary profiles. Root-height changes or disappearing contact alone establish none of these predicates.

A justified initial support contract needs complete identity/collision geometry, common metric transforms/gravity, a static-root role, a validated load-bearing witness (or validated force-free alternative), every indirect link and competing support exclusion, and a phase-qualified t0. A single-parent acyclic chain may be representable but no empirical indirect-support profile is validated here. Ambiguous/competing contacts, unsupported shapes or invalid bindings require abstention or affected-object IC as defined by the authoritative contract.
