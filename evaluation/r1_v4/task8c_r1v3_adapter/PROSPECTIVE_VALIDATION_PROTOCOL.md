# Proposed prospective validation protocol for Task 8C evidence extraction

**Status: proposal only; not an approved experimental procedure.** It supplements but does not replace or amend M4-v1.0, the frozen R1 v3 requirement/configuration/Checker, or existing Checker admission gates. No independent evaluation, additional annotation, or recording is authorized by this document.

## Method and target

Target domain: controlled externally induced support-loss events and matched stable controls for the designated protected object and source model. The primary target population is new, independently reset source executions using the frozen model, recorder, protected-object binding, and intervention procedure. Keep an externally induced fall distinct from a naturally occurring policy-execution failure. Task 8C used an external force pulse during stationary hold; it can test controlled event extraction but cannot establish policy-failure detection or natural-failure generalization. The current 20 cases and 10 source groups are development/regression data and are not an independent holdout.

The recovered `task8c-r1v3-source-adapter-1.0.0` checks recording, XML, native-map, entity, and runner bindings; full contact rows; body poses; frame and native-sample timing; action/sample alignment; and exact native body/geometry identity. It maps 258 saved observations to native samples `25 * frame` and does not use intermediate samples as checker frames. Geometry facts use the frozen XML local transforms and box-corner rule. Contact forces are preserved as recorded values but are not transformed into a load-bearing support claim.

## Supported evidence and required bindings

A future version may cite only values present in the source: source execution/group/case identifiers; HDF5 and XML hashes; model and native body/geometry IDs and names; saved observation frame and timestamp; per-sample native time and action index; body pose; contact geometry/body pair, point, signed distance, contact frame, force vector, and `ncon`; and reconstructed object/table collision vertices. Each output must retain source selector, source hash, native mapping hash, units/convention, and frame-to-sample derivation.

Missing contacts, rows, timestamps, body state, identities, complete frame coverage, exact runner source, or required uncertainty bounds remain missing and block the corresponding use. Do not synthesize policy actions, trajectory lineage, force interpretations, timestamps, physical labels, or contacts. Do not infer support solely from contact counts or infer falling solely from COM/center displacement. A controlled positive must have its external pulse recorded, source and runner provenance verified, and sufficient native measurements to annotate initial support, departure, downward motion, and (when the target event includes it) floor contact. A matched negative is a stable sham from the same source group and must meet the same scope and provenance checks. Human reviewers decide event applicability/outcome from blinded source evidence and do not provide inputs to the Checker or extractor.

## Unsupported conditions

This adapter supports only the frozen Task 8C schema, `cream_cheese_1`, its bound MuJoCo model, and the R1 saved-frame scope. It does not support other objects, object-to-object support chains whose transforms fail the frozen error bound, other recorder layouts, continuous-video claims between saved frames, natural-policy generalization, new event thresholds, or physical safety certification. RegistryV3 record schema 2.1.0 now supports the custom source selectors as development-only raw measurements; this does not alter the nine admission gates or enable empirical admission.

## Prospective independent evaluation design

Before collecting or scoring evaluation cases, preregister the target estimand and independent source group as the unit of resampling. Select new source executions after protocol freeze, separate from all Task 8C development cases and labels. Include paired controlled positive pulses and stable sham controls across independently reset source groups; include natural policy-execution cases only as a separately reported stratum when valid examples and complete provenance exist. The external intervention and policy action must be recorded distinctly.

The sample size is a protocol choice, not inherited from the earlier provisional 100-group suggestion. Predeclare precision for class-specific sensitivity and specificity at the independent source-group level (one positive and one matched stable control per group); frames and within-pair outcomes are not independent replicates. A conservative planning approximation at worst-case rate 0.5 is `n ~= 1.96^2 * 0.25 / half_width^2` independent observations per class: about 97 groups for a 95% half-width of 10 percentage points, 43 groups for 15 points, or 25 groups for 20 points. Use exact binomial intervals for class-specific rates and a source-group bootstrap for paired contrasts; recompute final sample sizes/coverage under the preregistered exact method and expected provenance attrition before collection. These are candidate designs, not approved targets.

Options for a one-week schedule are: (a) 12–20 new matched groups (24–40 recordings) as an engineering feasibility study, with wide uncertainty and no confirmatory performance claim; (b) 43 matched groups (86 recordings) as a precision-limited pilot targeting roughly ±15 percentage points per class; or (c) approximately 97 matched groups (194 recordings) for roughly ±10 points, likely infeasible in one week unless source executions and qualified reviewers are already available. A short one-week feasibility study is the recommended schedule option only if explicitly reported as exploratory; it cannot replace the larger confirmatory design. None may include already exposed Task 8C groups as independent holdout data.

Natural policy-execution failures, if available, require a separately selected and separately powered stratum with policy actions/trajectories and their provenance captured. Externally induced pulse cases cannot estimate natural-failure performance.

Two independent, blinded reviewers should inspect the prespecified raw evidence and classify applicability and event outcome separately, with event onset/offset or onset brackets, supporting saved-frame/native-sample references, and an indeterminate option. Reviewers must be blind to Checker outputs, pulse/sham assignment, and other reviewers’ decisions. Record reviewer eligibility/qualification, prior exposure to cohort/method/labels, independence declarations, and completion metadata. Resolve disagreements only under a predeclared adjudication rule and preserve both original judgments. Seal the complete prediction ledger and its hash before any reference is opened; retain a timestamped seal receipt and document access ordering. Bind each case to original recording and manifest hashes, model/XML, runner source, native mappings, protocol version, adapter version, Checker freeze, frame scope, exclusion reasons, and review receipt. Missing provenance makes a case ineligible; extractor abstentions remain in the results and are not dropped.

Report TP/FP/TN/FN and per-class sensitivity/specificity among eligible cases, applicability, decision coverage, abstentions, invalid records, provenance-unavailable cases, and exclusions separately, with numerators/denominators. Estimate uncertainty at source-group level: exact binomial intervals for each class rate and a paired source-group bootstrap interval for differences, retaining the two matched cases together. Stratify externally induced and natural policy cases; do not combine them to claim natural-failure performance. Admission still requires every existing gate and an authorized independent review; this protocol does not waive any gate.
