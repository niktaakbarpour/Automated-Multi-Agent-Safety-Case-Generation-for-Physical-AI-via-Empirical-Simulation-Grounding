## Why applicability stays blocked

A force-free support profile may eventually be defensible; forces are not universally mandatory. It must validate static-equilibrium assumptions, object constraints/external interactions, designated faces and all direct/indirect links, competing supports, precision and t0 phase. Contact presence, unchanged root height or image overlap alone cannot prove load-bearing support. Empty contact lists cannot become absence without an admitted complete support-exclusion/boundary contract.

Vertical AABB gap/contact-point proxies are reported descriptively. For rotated or side contacts they are not the signed normal separation. Fitting a tolerance to their extrema would not calibrate physical support. Existing V3 labels/rules were not used as targets. No initial-support applicability, support-loss or handling certificate is enabled.
