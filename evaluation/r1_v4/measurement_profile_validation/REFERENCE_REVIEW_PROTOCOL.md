## Evidence and dependence

Inspect the cited original trajectory hashes, exact native body/geom/contact/time paths and relevant pre/post/setup context. Match canonical/native identities and collision pieces before interpretation. Distinguish endpoint clocks, cache phases and camera exposure. New sheets outside Git are derived displays, not original measurements. Their hashes/raw-source paths are in CAMERA_EXCERPT_MANIFEST.json. They have not been visually reviewed in this phase.

The current proposals are a single AI-assisted analysis with prior conversation, audit and development-reference exposure. No fresh-context or independent AI-review claim is made. Overlapping cases and two cameras share original executions and producers; they are dependent, not replicated independent labels. Human review count and independent reference count remain zero. AI agreement cannot establish physical truth.

## Future review steps

1. Approve a development review/calibration protocol and reviewer exposure disclosures. Keep frozen NV/other held-out data excluded from tuning. Use a prospectively separate validation cohort.
2. Review measured facts and full context before reading AI hypotheses or any checker output. Preserve actual inspection coverage, sources/hashes and all timing/censoring limits. Camera access needs an authorized Hestia workflow; no image overlap alone establishes interaction.
3. Explicitly distinguish support, contact-only ambiguity, initially unsupported, sliding, lifting/holding, transport, support loss, downward movement, setup origin and post-lift release. Use unknown where a necessary condition lacks evidence. Record event brackets rather than invented exact times.
4. Independently justify any physical support alternative and uncertainty assumptions. Verify genuine R1 applicability/origin/unsupported motion/handling/interval predicates before labeling a positive. Do not promote setup departure or post-lift release merely to populate positives.
5. Preserve disagreements, dependency scopes and adjudication rationale. Lock source/reference/protocol hashes only after authorized independent review and approval. Do not score proposals as truth or compare V4 predictions to self-generated references.

Natural executions and future controlled perturbation scenarios must remain separate. This task authorizes neither new experiments nor empirical accuracy/certification claims. Reference eligibility for absent objects and any broader episode policy remain user protocol decisions.
