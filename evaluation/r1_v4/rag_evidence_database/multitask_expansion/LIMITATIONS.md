# Release limitations

This release indexes 285 of the 2,000 inventoried executions. Metadata-only
historical catalog coverage is not original-measurement coverage. Remaining
partitions require reader-profile validation and fresh source hashes; the 60
reserved evaluations and 38 historical preflights are not development data.

Verified recording identities, array structures and source declarations are not
calibrated physical predicates. Native geometry admission, initial support,
fall witnesses and negative certificates remain blocked/unsupported as previously
documented. Contact is not load-bearing support; a root-height change is not a
fall. Camera exposure and endpoint/cache timing remain uncertain. No new human
reference labels or physical safety conclusions are issued.

CHANNEL_COVERAGE.md lists saved fields without separate retrieval units and
unverified raw observable conventions. The six indexed measurement families do
not imply that every archived file or numerical channel is independently indexed.
Robot/camera/contact payloads can be shared contextual references under multiple
object scopes, with source dependencies preserved.

Coverage is saved-frame availability over the query's valid matches. Use an
explicit execution and object scope for per-execution gap interpretation.
Broad multi-execution queries do not provide per-execution completeness or
qualified physical coverage. All coverage validation queries in this phase are
explicitly execution/object scoped. No cross-source physical coverage composition
or empirical negative certificate is enabled.

Metadata and BDDL instruction wording differ by 'pick up' versus 'Pick'. Both
versions are attributed separately; instruction chunks are recorder metadata,
not verbatim BDDL clauses. No semantic equivalence is asserted by the registry.

The optional semantic index covers six raw measurement descriptions only;
documents, task-instruction paraphrases and AI reviews are not embedded. It
requires at most 100 valid candidates after metadata filtering. Unembedded IDs
are reported, not presented as returned semantic evidence. Scores are source-text
similarities, not confidence in measurements or safety. The comparison repeats
12 hand-written paraphrases across execution/task scopes, so 144 query runs are
not 144 independent language-relevance cases. Broader human-judged retrieval
relevance remains future database-validation work, distinct from physical ground
truth. Source-target lookup metrics must not be extrapolated to safety accuracy.

Historical records are preserved exactly, including their original timestamp
coordinate-frame labels. Newly indexed timestamp-only records explicitly declare
a nonspatial frame. VALIDATION_HISTORY.json records the initially failing test
and corrected rebuild; no failed candidate database is the validated release.

The immutable Phase5D baseline also retains legacy execution-summary text saying
'Development only' for its 49 expansion cases. Their machine-readable
corpus_expansion stratum and development_only=false provenance are authoritative;
the legacy summary sentence does not authorize calibration use. Phase5E corrects
the factory for newly built records and preserves all baseline rows exactly.
Use explicit stratum filters and provenance rather than an unqualified text
match to enforce data-use boundaries. This inherited wording limitation is not
silently relabeled as a verified development permission.
