# Phase 5E measurement-only scope

The SOURCE_PLAN.json fixes four task partitions, each with 49 nonreserved
executions. Selection sorts the remaining single-XML, archive-mapped candidate
partitions by authoritative nine-file recording size, then partition name.
No task success, safety label, V3 prediction or AI event classification is used.
The source plan records checkpoint, task, seed, execution, archive member,
expected source hash and destination separately for every member.

All four tasks use the recorder's schema 4.0 contract, but native mappings are
compiled separately from each task's hash-bound simulator XML and verified
assets. Anonymous native geometry IDs are retained. Shape dimensions, object
body ownership, static fixture geometry, cached rigid transforms and recording
identity are checked independently. MuJoCo model compilation performs no physics
steps, rendering or new experiment. The reader checks every saved frame even
when its caller requests a subset for inspection.

The task profiles authorize measurement availability only. Static fixture
references do not establish an object-to-support relation. BOX or other native
shape declarations do not calibrate collision bounds. Contact cache positions
and signed distances are recorded observations; forces and normals are absent.
Root pose does not establish falling. Saved endpoint timestamps, last-forward
geometry/contact caches and camera exposures do not establish identical physical
instants. Camera exposure alignment and empirical support/fall/negative admission
remain disabled or blocked. Saved-frame availability is not continuous physical
coverage.

The original 40 development executions remain in the development stratum. The
49 previous and 196 new expansion executions have corpus_expansion stratum,
development_only=false and unclassified_database_use_only study partition.
This is database-use permission, not calibration or reference-label permission.
All 60 reserved evaluations and 38 historical cross-scene preflight executions
are excluded. AI reviews retained from the baseline remain separately attributed
provisional interpretations. No human reference judgments are manufactured.
