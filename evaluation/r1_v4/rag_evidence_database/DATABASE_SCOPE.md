# Scope and coverage plan

Phase 5B constructs and validates the database only. Parent eefa9c36607ece47f0d8a57718a1d87d9969d07f. No drafter, critic, revision agent, GSN generation, new checker or empirical classifier is implemented.

Initial population: the four verified development executions 000, 001, 002 and 014 of OpenVLA-OFT LIBERO Long task_00. All 1,312 saved frames and all five reader-supported explicitly identified objects are indexed without applicability/event filtering. The 32-frame window size is an indexing parameter, not a physical persistence threshold. The complete native map retains 161 IDs. Other scene objects remain discoverable in model identity sources; per-object measurement validation is bounded to the five existing bindings.

The project inventory contains 2,000 executions across 40 tasks. Its split contains 40 proposed development executions and ten held-out executions; all ten held-out IDs overlap the 20 frozen NV cases. Disjoint catalog classifications are 40 proposed development, 20 frozen NV and 1,940 unclassified, not automatically development. The split remains historically proposed, not human-approved. Four development executions have restored validated raw sources; 36 require selective restoration and reader-profile extension.

After development validation, expansion adds all 2,000 project entries to a separately labeled historical metadata catalog, including unavailable and potentially non-applicable recordings. Catalog entries never substitute for original measurements. They contain identities, instructions, expected hashes, archive references and partition flags, not physical labels. Frozen/held-out measurements and reference labels are not ingested or used to tune search.

Transfer receipts identify 15,361 LIBERO trajectory paths totaling 2,766,491,538,022 bytes. 10,592 such paths remain under owned /disk/1 roots, by filesystem existence only. These are file path counts, not deduplicated freshly validated execution IDs. Their model/recorder contracts, completeness, splits and hashes have not been validated for this reader. They remain inventory-only. Historical published_verified Alliance receipts establish archive mappings; remote liveness was not rechecked. No archive was restored.

Budget: estimated development index 32 MiB, initial working allowance 128 MiB, /disk/1 checked for a 500 GB reserve. /disk/1 had about 1.66 TB available; /disk/0 had only 385.94 GB and received no generated artifacts. Final database: 14,622,720 bytes including the catalog. No entire-corpus completeness claim is made.
