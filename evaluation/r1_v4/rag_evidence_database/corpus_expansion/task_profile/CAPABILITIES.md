# New-task capability scope

Verified for the selected source set: exact source bytes; task/model metadata attestations; schema/shape/dtype and frame/transition consistency; complete compiler-resolved native identities/ownership and joint addresses; rigid cached body/geometry consistency; recorded pose/contact/native-state/timestamp availability; camera-array references. Existing model weights are not rehashed or run. Source assets and compiler version are pinned, and compilation independently reproduces.

Conditional/qualified: units and coordinate declarations follow the recorder/model contract; raw-observable frame/quaternion conventions and cache phase remain explicit uncertainties. Static floor geometry is a source identity, not proof of load-bearing support. Source masks identify collision capability, not actual support/contact force. State/geometry/camera records are not assumed simultaneous exposures.

Unsupported or blocked: contact normals and per-contact forces (not saved); camera exposure alignment; calibrated physical geometry bounds for the new plane/non-BOX profile; empirical initial support, falling witnesses, qualified negatives and dynamic coverage composition. No tolerance, alignment window, persistence or confidence cutoff is invented. Source-count coverage never becomes physical coverage.

Seven source-declared objects are indexed, not silently designated as R1 protected scope. New records are corpus_expansion with development_only=false and unclassified_database_use_only; all reserved/preflight examples remain excluded. AI reviews stay separate and provisional. No physical event or safety judgment is produced.
