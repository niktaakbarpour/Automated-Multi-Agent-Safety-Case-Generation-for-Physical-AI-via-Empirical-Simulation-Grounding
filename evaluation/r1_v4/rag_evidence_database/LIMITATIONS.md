# Scientific and coverage limitations

This is a validated development database plus a historical metadata catalog, not a complete LIBERO measurement corpus. Four execution payloads, one task and five explicit object bindings are measurement-indexed. Broader local trajectory existence and verified archival receipts are inventory facts; missing originals are never replaced with summaries.

No verified in-scope R1 positive exists in the prior work. Initial load-bearing support, fall witnesses, qualified negatives, physical uncertainty calibration and independent references remain blocked. Geometry/contact physical admission remains conditional. Camera exposure alignment is unresolved. Contact alone, root height or image appearance cannot establish support/falling. No uncertainty threshold is invented. Source hashes prove byte identity, not physical truth.

Window availability covers recorded indices only, not unsampled physics or continuous observation. The frame-domain API rejects incompatible time domains and does not compose dynamic coverage. All six measurement categories share source dependencies; duplicate views are not independent evidence.

The development split is historically proposed and exposed to AI review. Ten AI interpretations are not human ground truth. Frozen/held-out measurement labels are excluded from indexing and retrieval tuning; identity/partition metadata are retained only for source coverage. Human forms remain unchanged.

The 69 evaluated queries have known source targets on a small purposive one-task corpus. Their relevance judgments are not physical annotations, independent semantic evaluation or safety accuracy. Vector search, natural paraphrase validation, new reader/model contracts and broad scale execution remain future database work. Current search caps require narrower metadata filters at scale.

No safety-case agent, drafter, critic, revision process, new GSN graph generation, checker version or empirical detector was implemented. No simulation, original-file modification, archive restoration, push or merge occurred.
