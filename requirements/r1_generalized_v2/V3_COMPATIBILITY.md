## Non-comparable conditions and required regressions

Different P/S, task/scene/model/identity bindings, interval anchors, geometry/clock
contracts, handling/re-placement policies, reference provenance, coverage domains,
calibration or aggregation policies require a separately versioned evaluation.
Never reuse old TNs under a broader claim. A stricter or more permissive contract
can change eligibility without indicating improved physical detection.

Freeze a prospective V4 protocol and tests from [V4_TEST_MATRIX.md](V4_TEST_MATRIX.md):
legacy byte/result preservation; initial-applicability gating of event tags; boundary
setup/landing history; abstention and disjoint-domain handling; new surface identity;
missing/unsupported inputs; object-count invariance; source tamper and calibration
drift rejection. Any genuine behavior change is a reported V4 condition, not a V3 fix.
