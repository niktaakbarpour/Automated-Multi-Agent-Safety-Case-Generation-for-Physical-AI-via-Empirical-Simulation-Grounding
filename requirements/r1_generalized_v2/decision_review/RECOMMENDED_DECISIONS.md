Actual-register versus preference differences: D01 explicitly includes task targets
and verified absence as NA; D02 proposes a *union per object*, indirect chains and
handoffs, beyond merely multiple tables across objects; D03 asks approval of the
historical post-lift exclusion, not just intention; D05 explicitly orders IC versus
unknown versus positive; D06 includes post-boundary look-ahead; D08 includes carry
and excluded-phase negative composition. The matrix addresses these actual questions.
No preference is treated as authorization for a specific implementation or numerical
calibration. In particular, “intentionally removed” is not a physics category: a
carried removal and a deliberately pushed unsupported fall are different events.

Recommended abstract requirement wording remains the Phase 1 intent:

> For each explicitly protected object with established direct or qualified indirect
> support by its designated support system at the declared start, prevent verified
> unsupported downward falling departure originating from that support within the
> monitored interval. Preserve the established lift/carry exclusion and report all
> scope, evidence and coverage limitations.

This paraphrase does not replace normative text. Exact proposed amendments are in
[PROPOSED_AMENDMENTS.md](PROPOSED_AMENDMENTS.md), with an explicit consistency map.
No scope expansion, empirical performance or independent human validation follows
from accepting it. Do not reinterpret the old 20 cases under the new requirement.

Recommended sequence after review: record actual user dispositions, integrate only
approved amendments into a separately reviewed specification revision, revalidate
schema/formal text/test expectations, then seek authorization for restricted V4
contracts and analytical fixtures. Physical profiles stay gated on evidence. This
task stops at the review package.
