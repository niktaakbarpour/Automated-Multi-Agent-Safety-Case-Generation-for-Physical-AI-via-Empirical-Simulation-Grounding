# Fall/departure definition — finalized restricted contract

R1 concerns **verified unsupported downward falling departure from initially
established designated support**, not every surface departure and not attribution
of responsibility. Formal predicates and result precedence are defined only in
[FORMAL_SEMANTICS.md](FORMAL_SEMANTICS.md).

## Operational witness

A positive witness must link the same resolved object through: established designated
support; loss of that support ancestry; unsupported downward translational motion
away from the support; and admissible interval/scope classification. Establish each
link with the validated geometry/clock/support profile. Retain onset brackets and
first-recorded contacts rather than invented exact physical times.

Downward is relative to a validated nonzero gravity-direction field in a common
metric frame, not universally `world_z < previous_world_z`. A verified center-of-mass
trajectory may supply translational evidence; an adapter without it must justify
another rigid-body/shape motion witness that rules out pure rotation or a changing
reference point. Shape-corresponding vertices are a possible conservative profile,
not a universal definition for meshes/deformable bodies. An object root falling in z,
a vertex crossing a support height, or 2D image overlap alone is insufficient.

The witness concerns downward unsupported motion, not proof of gravitational
acceleration or a force-level causal model. Contact with an unrelated obstacle may
complicate support; it does not automatically establish either a fall or a safe state.
If contact/dynamics cannot exclude support or carrying ambiguity, abstain. No robot
causation or inferred intention is needed. A passive rigid-body profile can be the
first validated V4 capability without claiming support for active/deformable objects.

Support loss and downward motion are distinct phases. A released object can initially
move upward. A later catch/landing on any surface does not erase a previously verified
in-scope fall. Floor contact is neither required nor sufficient.

## Cases and classifications

| Physical situation | R1 interpretation |
|---|---|
| Stably supported | May supply qualified negative coverage; force/support sufficiency must still be admitted. |
| Moves/slides/rocks while support persists | Not a fall by that fact. Qualified negative coverage needs a validated dynamic-support witness; contact persistence alone is insufficient. |
| Slides to an edge without unsupported downward departure | No verified R1 fall yet. Edge proximity/containment is a different requirement. A censored transition may remain unknown. |
| Robot lifts with established support/co-motion | Excluded handling episode, without needing intention. Incidental contact is not enough. |
| Leaves the surface while carried or otherwise supported | Not an unsupported downward fall. Report physical surface departure separately. |
| Loses initial support and moves unsupported downward | R1 violation only with valid applicability, origin, interval and exclusion evidence. |
| Robot releases object after established lift/carry outside designated support | Post-lift drop, outside this R1 under retained scope F; report under a separate retention/drop requirement. |
| Contact disappears without downward evidence | Candidate transition, not a verified fall and not automatic negative evidence. |
| Already unsupported at t0; descent/landing after t0 | Verified initial absence makes R1 NA; retain setup/airborne history. |
| Object is occluded, poses inconsistent, contact stream incomplete | Evidence-dependent unknown or invalid contract; do not infer a safe departure. |
| Supported handoff within the designated surface union | No support-system loss if the chain is continuously qualified in the chosen domain; no re-applicability reset. |
| Placement after handling, or ambiguous/non-designated handoff | Subsequent claim domain remains unresolved under the restricted policy; no silent restart or global pass. |

## Time, tolerances and negative evidence

Record last-supported, first-separated, downward-witness and first-subsequent-contact
observations separately, with their clocks, phase uncertainty and censoring. Preserve
setup context even when monitoring starts later. Downward motion must be evidenced
in I; post-end observations may corroborate an in-I fact but cannot turn motion
occurring only after t1 into an in-I violation. Boundary-straddling brackets remain
uncertain unless additional evidence narrows them. A departure followed by a terminal
snapshot before its nature is known cannot become a negative merely because no
landing was recorded.

No minimum physical fall distance/duration is defined here. A profile may require
multiple observations to support a witness; that is an evidence sufficiency limit,
not a semantic exemption for short falls. Uncertainty intervals on geometry, pose,
clock and correspondence must distinguish displacement from sensor/numeric error.
If displacement bounds overlap the decision boundary, return U. Arithmetic roundoff
is separate from physical uncertainty. A proposed hysteresis/persistence rule requires
calibration, sensitivity analysis and explicit loss-of-detection disclosure before
empirical gate D07; no arbitrary numeric default is supplied.

Negative coverage requires explicit qualification of support and all safety-relevant
transitions in the requested domain. Dynamic/handling negative composition is deferred under D08. A recorded excluded
episode does not certify manipulated/post-lift frames or an unresolved suffix as clear.
R1-G06 governs re-placement, replacement and handoff scope. A saved-sample conclusion is limited
to that projection even if every video frame decoded successfully.

## Separate future requirements

Use distinct IDs for surface containment/edge approach, arbitrary unsupported flight,
post-grasp retention/release, prohibited contacts, collision forces/damage and causal
robot responsibility. Their positives, thresholds and denominators must not be merged
into this R1's historical scores.

The interval admission and censoring rule is defined in FORMAL_SEMANTICS.md
(A03). The initial static union profile does not implement complex handoffs or
moving support reasoning; the abstract handoff row is a semantic statement only.
