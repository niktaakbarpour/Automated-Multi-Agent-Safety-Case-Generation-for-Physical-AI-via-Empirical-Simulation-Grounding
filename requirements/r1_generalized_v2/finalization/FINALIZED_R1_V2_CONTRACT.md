# Finalized R1 2.0.0 restricted contract

Design finalized; empirical admission pending. Authority and D01–D11 limitations are
in [DECISION_REGISTER.md](DECISION_REGISTER.md). Normative meaning and precedence
remain in [R1_V2_SPECIFICATION.md](../R1_V2_SPECIFICATION.md) and
[FORMAL_SEMANTICS.md](../FORMAL_SEMANTICS.md), with their referenced evidence,
applicability and fall rules. This summary does not override those definitions.

R1 is prevention of an admitted unsupported downward falling departure originating
from each explicitly protected object's established designated support at t0, within
the fixed requested interval. Sliding/rotation, established carrying and linked
post-lift drops are distinct. No causation, floor landing, minimum fall distance or
duration is added. Re-placement/replacement/handoff does not re-arm the claim.

Configuration failure is rejected before assessment with configuration_status=invalid,
assessed=false, object_assessments=[], execution_outcome=null and diagnostics.
Otherwise retain every object, applicability, per-claim contract status, requested
and qualified domains, conflicts, excluded episodes, uncertainty and outcome reasons.
Verified absence is NA only under an admitted absence certificate, never a safety
pass. Unknown presence is U; unsupported essential measurement is IC.

Only witnesses surviving identity/applicability/origin/scope/clock/geometry/provenance
and conflict admission can be positive. A valid violation is decisive even with
unrelated NA/U/IC; report completeness separately. Aggregate admitted outcomes by
V > IC > U > qualified N > NA. Zero scope is explicit and excluded from metrics.
Full negative requires every requested cell and relevant join transition qualified;
no holes, unresolved contradictions or unresolved scope suffix may be filled by union.

Configuration schema 2.0.1 requires manifest 1.0.0 and approved_restricted_design.
The manifest pins R1 2.0.0 and independent config/evidence/adapter/profile/checker
versions and hashes, purpose, capability/calibration/source/model records and domain.
Runtime must resolve and validate scope/compatibility; fixture nulls and flags admit
nothing. Evidence/checker schema artifacts must be created and versioned in later
implementation; none is falsely marked implemented here.

Permitted engineering scope is [RESTRICTED_V4_SCOPE.md](RESTRICTED_V4_SCOPE.md).
Empirical use remains subject to [EMPIRICAL_GATES.md](EMPIRICAL_GATES.md).
No implementation, simulations, RAG, multi-agent system, historical relabeling,
publication, push or merge is performed in this phase.
