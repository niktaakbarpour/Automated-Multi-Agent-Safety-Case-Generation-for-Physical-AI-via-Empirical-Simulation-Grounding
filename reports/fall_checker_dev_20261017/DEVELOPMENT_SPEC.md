# Development specification and qualifications

The pending requirement is retained in requirement_v2.json. Its approved-objective provenance does not approve the requirement. Scope configuration is PROPOSED_UNAPPROVED and must be reviewed separately from human example labels and detector validation.

**Interval.** Frame 10, the saved state immediately before the first policy-issued action after ten settling commands, through the final post-action snapshot. Frame i+11 corresponds to policy action i. Frames 0–10 are separately considered for setup anomalies; setup event history is reset at frame 10 so a later floor landing does not relocate an earlier departure into the policy interval. Task success has no role. All five objects are retained per development run; all held-out run identities are reported as not processed.

**Identity and phase.** Each run must be development-only, match the task/run HDF5 attributes and XML SHA, have complete frame/action counts, correct native geometry count and coherent body/geometry transforms under the v2 arithmetic guard. Contacts are resolved by native ID including anonymous table boxes. Missing/invalid bundles break event chains and produce uncertainty, never an automatic pass. Endpoint qpos and raw observations are not used as synchronous substitutes. Recorded derived geometry and contact bundles are used together at their qualified nominal phase t−2 ms (except frame zero). Raw fields may lag a further forward phase; images lack independent exposure timestamps. Current dependency-source evidence has unverified historical identity, while producer-hash-verified recorder source is distinguished in the copied provenance. The terminal endpoint's last 2 ms is not recovered by changing poses. No static replacement trajectory or physics integration is performed.

**Initial support.** Per object: collision-box bottom and recorded contact position near the upper face of table collision patches 10–14, contact within patch bounds and no excessive penetration, yield a direct support *candidate*. Normals/forces are absent: this is not verified weight support. Contact with another non-table body yields an unresolved indirect support candidate, not established support through a table-supported object. Side contacts never establish top support. No table support at frame 10 is `initial_table_support_not_established`, not a protected object that passed and not verified non-applicability. No indirect-support inference is invented. Missing/ambiguous evidence returns unknown.

**Observations and events.** Full recorded collision poses and native contact bundles are retained in compressed derived exports. Per-object bounds, support/side-contact candidates, possible indirect contacts and gripper interactions are exported per frame. A table-support candidate establishes candidate departure history. Two successive sufficiently descending AABB-center samples without contact produce an unsupported-descent candidate, classified by history: direct table departure; post-lift drop when bilateral gripper contact and collision-bottom clearance preceded release; or unknown origin. This last category explicitly preserves initial airborne/setup cases. AABB-center descent can include rotation and requires review; it is not COM acceleration or proof of a ballistic fall.

Supported tipping/sliding does not become a fall solely because a point moves down. Departure plus descent without intervening unresolved contact can be direct-table-fall candidate evidence only when collision bounds also leave the table support projection or lie wholly below its upper surface; otherwise the table-support-loss descent remains ambiguous; unobserved inter-sample catches and support remain possible. Lifting/carrying is provisional mechanical evidence without intent. Post-lift drops—including episode 014 if detected—remain a separate type and have no automatic original-requirement violation. Contacts on any surface may end descent, including a catch, a table return or another object. Floor contact is not required. Events remain in the record after catch/recovery. Ambiguous gripper-only interactions without a defensible supported lift cannot prove direct-fall origin; these require review.

**Interpretation categories reserved for reviewed checker decisions.** (1) Observed violation requires reviewed applicability, fall definition, evidence and validated detector. (2) No violation observed can describe only stated sampled coverage, never continuous safety. (3) Insufficient information covers missing/ambiguous support, synchronization, scope and event evidence. (4) Verified non-applicability requires a verified reason; unknown initial support is insufficient. This development release does not assign final categories or safety verdicts. It reports physical candidates and pending requirement assessment.

**Known limitations.** Twenty Hz endpoint observations miss substep events and cannot establish continuous support. The two-interval candidate filter misses short events and can split contact-interrupted descent. Missing contact normals/forces prevent verified support or grasp. AABB projection is conservative for object volume but center motion is not a material point; rotation can create descent candidates. The model-specific table code explicitly rejects nonhorizontal patches and non-box protected collision geometry instead of silently applying invalid approximations. Detector sensitivity is not precision/recall; independent labels and future held-out evaluation are still required.
