# Explicit proposed descent and event rules

Threshold values, five objects, 40/10 partition, phase qualifications and requirement scope are unchanged. All choices remain unapproved development configuration.

A counted interval joins two consecutive valid frames with the expected 0.05 s simulator endpoint spacing. **Both endpoints** must have no recorded external contact, no direct-table-support candidate, no unresolved indirect support and no gripper contact. AABB-center decrease across that interval must meet the existing descent_m parameter. Two consecutive qualifying intervals therefore require **three contact-free samples**. Supported frame 10 followed by descending free frames 11 and 12 supplies only one qualifying interval, 11→12. Frame 13 can confirm a second interval if it also qualifies. Endpoint qualification does not prove contact absence between samples, free fall, COM descent or a safety violation.

The support-departure bracket is the consecutive supported/non-supported frame pair and is stored separately from confirmation_window and descent_frames. It is not counted as a contact-free interval when the earlier endpoint has support/contact. Confirmation frame is immutable. Unconfirmed descent sequences are exported with their stopping reason, including short drops that return to contact before persistence is met.

Each active event retains initial_classification and classification_history. Later whole-collision-below-tabletop or outside-support-projection evidence can strengthen table_support_loss_descent_ambiguous_candidate to direct_table_departure_descent_candidate. Evidence has exact frame/time references and is accumulated through ending. Unresolved pre-event contact history, gripper ambiguity and unknown origin are not cleared by later geometry evidence; post-lift drops remain a separate type. A catch or recovery ends an observed segment without deleting its evidence or type history. Floor landing is never required.

observed_event_span runs from the known non-supported endpoint of the departure bracket, or the first qualifying contact-free sample when departure is unknown, through the last valid observed frame, including an observed contact ending. The confirmation_window is distinct and uses only the required descent intervals. Subsequent qualifying intervals are stored individually; a non-descending endpoint is not silently counted. The event remains active until a contact transition, table recovery, gap, policy boundary or recording termination. Ending records state whether the observation is censored. Gaps/boundaries reset origin history for later candidates. Setup and policy segments are not joined; unknown origin is never transformed into a verified applicability decision.

Per-frame construction stages all object/profile calculations and validates them before appending. A calculation failure invalidates the whole frame transaction across all profiles. The commit validates every destination before any mutation and rejects duplicate/out-of-order frame IDs. Exactly one record is committed per object/profile/frame, including invalid records. Exceptions and invalid frames break detection chains; they do not become passes.

Geometry/contact evidence remains at the v2-qualified derived phase, nominally t−2 ms relative to simulator endpoint time; raw camera exposure phase is unverified. No poses are reconstructed or replaced. Twenty Hz endpoint coverage can miss intervening events and cannot establish continuous safety. Initial support, physical event labels and requirement-violation decisions are separate reviews.
