# M4 final experiment registry and evidence boundary

**Snapshot:** `main` at `2bbfbc19dc3678b8334ef510115eaac26a0eb6a3` (PR #47 merge), verified through the connected GitHub repository. This document is an internal evidence registry, not manuscript prose. Counts below keep evaluation cohorts separate. Private, unfinished RQ2 reviews, AI-review outputs and sealed RQ3 review material were not opened.

## Admission classes

- **Development-only:** saved results may be reported descriptively with their exposure, grouping and denominator limits; they are not independent test performance.
- **Coverage-only:** report eligibility, coverage, abstention and exclusion counts only. There are no reference labels for accuracy.
- **Pending:** no result is admitted until the relevant independent references are finalized, verified and frozen.
- **Blocked / NO-GO:** the planned experiment lacks eligible empirical inputs or an approved protocol; structural tests do not fill that gap.
- **Historical/exploratory:** a frozen, exposed analysis may be reported as a bounded diagnostic, with no generalization claim.
